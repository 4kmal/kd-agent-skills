# Paper collage style guide

**Palette (sheets, one per scene):** newsprint `#ece5d3`, mustard `#f2c14e`, pale blue `#a8d5e2`, pink `#f4b6c2`, off-white `#f4efe4`, signal red `#e63946`. Pieces: off-white `#fbf8f0`, kraft `#c8a46e`, ink `#141414`, sticky yellow `#f7e26b`. Marker red `#e63946`, pen blue `#1d4ed8`.

**Type:** Playfair Display 900 for newspaper headlines; Special Elite (typewriter) for datelines, URLs and subheads; Barlow Condensed 800 for strips and labels; Bungee for index-card labels; Caveat 700 for handwriting; JetBrains Mono for terminals. Ransom letters mix all of them.

**Composition (1080x1920):**
- Keep the top 150 px for the masthead tag and the bottom 140 px for the footer strip.
- Big headline in the top third, the main object in the middle, the punchline or annotation low.
- Every piece is slightly rotated (−6° to +6°); nothing sits perfectly level except the terminal text.
- Overlap pieces like a real collage, but never cover a caption or a word the voice is saying.
- Tape goes across a corner or the top edge, rotated against the piece.

**Motion:**
- Pieces are *slapped* down (scale 1.18 → 1, a few degrees of spin) on the word that names them, with a paper, snip or tape sound.
- New scenes slide in as a full sheet from alternating sides.
- Marker strokes draw over 0.4–0.6 s; ransom letters pop one by one 50–70 ms apart.
- Everything animates on twos (15 fps) and boils at 12 fps; do not add smooth easing that fights it.

**Sound:** a quiet lo-fi bed (84 BPM, soft keys, vinyl crackle) 18–20 dB under the voice; every visible action has a small paper-world sound.

**Honesty:** collage invites "fake newspaper" looks. Use real facts only, mark opinions as the narrator's, and credit screenshots and sources in the caption.
